How to build it

Works offline. Finds the closed outline of your drawing and puffs it into a solid, like a cookie or a cushion. Best for one clear shape drawn with a dark pen.

Sends the photo to Claude, which reads the drawing (and any dimensions written on it) and builds the model from simple parts: boxes, spheres, cylinders, cones and rings. Uses your own Anthropic API key and costs a few cents per sketch.

The key goes only from your browser to api.anthropic.com. Get one at console.anthropic.com.
